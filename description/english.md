Enter the deep forests with **Preserve**, a woodland town inspired by the legend of *The Lord of the Rings*. Its people live alongside elves and forest creatures, defending their realm through patience, magic, and the strength of the wild.

Preserve armies grow from humble **Gnomes** and **Wolves** into **Tigers**, plant-like **Waspworts**, and swift **Mantises**. **Forest Druids** and their greater kin lend the town its magic, while **Forest Dragons** and **Sprite Dragons** command the highest tier of the army. The town also fields special creatures such as **Ravens**, **Sun Birds**, and Preserve Fairies.

The town follows the familiar Heroes III building progression, from the **Gnome Village** and **Wolf Den** to the **Golden Loft**. Its distinctive structures include the **Druid's Academy**, where heroes can study secondary skills, the **Rainbow**, which grants visiting heroes luck, and the **Spirit of Life**, which strengthens the whole realm.

Magic is closely tied to the forest. Preserve units and heroes use effects such as **Forest Power**, **Scout**, **Symbiosis**, and **Power Barrier** to disrupt enemies, empower allies, and control the course of battle.

---

Preserve uses **Grass** as its native terrain.
